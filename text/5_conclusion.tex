\chapter*{Conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion}

This thesis investigated feature selection methods in the context of neural networks and high-dimensional data. The theoretical part introduced the main concepts of neural networks and feature selection, while the practical part evaluated selected methods on synthetic datasets with different signal structures.

The results showed that feature selection can improve predictive performance and reduce training time by removing irrelevant predictors. However, the effectiveness of individual methods depends strongly on the underlying data structure, particularly in the presence of nonlinear relationships.
