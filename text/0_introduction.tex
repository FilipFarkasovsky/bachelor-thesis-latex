\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}


The neural network is a supervised machine learning model inspired by the biological neural networks of the human brain. Because of their ability to capture complex patterns, they are widely used in areas such as image recognition, language processing, and bioinformatics. However, their performance can decline when datasets contain irrelevant or redundant features. Large datasets with many features require longer training times and are harder to interpret, which is a problem often referred to as the \textit{curse of dimensionality}. To cope with this problem, methods of feature selection are implemented.

Feature selection is the process of identifying the most relevant features in a dataset while removing those that do not contribute significantly to predictive performance. By reducing the number of input variables, we can improve model accuracy, speed up training, and make the results easier to interpret. 

The main goal of this thesis is to explore different feature selection methods in the context of neural networks. The work will include a theoretical comparison of these approaches and a practical demonstration on a selected dataset, demonstrating how different methods influence model performance and interpretability.

The structure of the thesis is as follows: Chapter 1 discusses the importance of neural networks and feature selection. Chapter 2 introduces neural network fundamentals and basic concepts of feature selection. Chapter 3 reviews the main feature selection methods. Chapter 4 presents a practical application, including data preprocessing, model training, and evaluation. 

This thesis combines theoretical review with practical experiments to demonstrate the importance of feature selection in neural networks and its impact on model performance.

The source code used in the practical part is included in the appendix and was implemented using Python libraries.
